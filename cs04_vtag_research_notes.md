## Emerging story angles for CS04
1. **Analysis**: Health Canada compliance, bilingual copy load, barcode / symbol / ingredient constraints, manufacturing coordination.
2. **Design**: modular packaging architecture to preserve clarity under warning-heavy layouts.
3. **Development**: repeatable template system across starter kit, device kit, pod kit, and disposable lines.
4. **Implementation**: rollout across print, product pages, manuals, B2B/B2C collateral, and production specs.
5. **Evaluation**: iteration via typo checks, translation checks, fit/finish adjustments, material and color corrections.

## Additional findings from EON_300_single_Design_HUB_08262021_wip04.pdf (pages 1-4 viewed)
- EON single-device packaging uses a highly standardized front architecture across flavor variants.
- Observed flavor pages: Mint/Menthe, Blue Lemon/Citron Bleu, Mango/Mangue, Blue Razz/Framboise Bleue.
- Consistent structure across variants:
  - top bilingual flavor banner
  - front pack with V-TAG wordmark and central product render
  - nicotine strength strip near lower front panel
  - mandatory bilingual warning block on front
  - side panel showing bilingual flavor name vertically
  - right panel with contents-in-box, ingredients, poison warning, barcode, and manufacturing statement
- Important narrative value:
  - one design system extends across many SKUs without losing compliance clarity
  - bilingual naming affects line length and spacing decisions directly
  - warning density forces careful hierarchy and spatial compression
  - packaging functions as both brand surface and regulated information container
- This supports a strong CS04 section on modular systems, translation fit, and iterative template refinement.
