01  ·  Understand

Every project begins with understanding the material — what it resists, what it allows. Before a brief, before a client, there is a question about form and space. That question is the same whether the material is clay, paper, or a screen.

The work here is not background. It is the foundation the rest is built on.

The Method

The process does not change. The material does.

Understand the problem. Research the constraints. Sketch the possibilities. Design the system. Apply it to the real world. Evaluate what holds.

This sequence is not a formula — it is a discipline. It applies to a ceramic vessel, a regulated package, a spatial environment, or a digital interface. The medium changes. The rigour does not.
