I’m a graphic designer specialising in visual communication, with 10+ years of experience spanning branding, editorial, packaging, education, and UI/UX across Toronto and Seoul.

My path runs from traditional craft to digital — trained first in ceramic art at Kyung Hee University, then graphic design at OCAD University — so I see beyond pixels to hand-crafted experiences. I thrive under pressure, know when to push and when to stop, and build long-lasting impressions through calm, considered design.

I’m currently designing for kindergartens in South Korea, leading projects that help kids, parents, and teachers manage their daily routines and schedules — combining strategy and creative direction to make school enjoyable. The same end-to-end brand thinking I bring to every client project.

Currently open to full-time opportunities in Toronto.
