# Case Study 03 — AZOTO & Pure6 (UKIN)

## 1. Hero & Meta
- **Hero Image:** azoto_sign_glow.png (Illuminated AZOTO logo) — Dark, dramatic, high contrast
- **Client:** UKIN
- **Role:** Design Lead (Founding Member)
- **Scope:** Brand Identity, Trademark, Packaging, Environmental Design, Side-Project Art Direction
- **Year:** 2014–2015

## 2. Context (UKIN)
- **Heading:** Building a Food Brand Incubator
- **Body:** As a founding design member of UKIN — an F&B startup incubator that grew from seed to Series B — the challenge was to build brands from zero. The flagship project was **AZOTO**, a liquid nitrogen ice cream concept. There was no name, no logo, and no physical space. The mandate was to create a total brand experience that could scale across multiple franchise locations.

## 3. Brand Identity (AZOTO)
- **Heading:** From Geometry to Typography
- **Body:** The identity for AZOTO was built from the ground up. Starting with the basic geometric forms of an ice cream cone (a circle and a triangle), the logo evolved into a custom typographic mark. The inverted triangles formed the letter 'A', creating a sharp, scientific aesthetic that matched the -196°C liquid nitrogen concept.
- **Images:** 
  - AZOTO_0.webp (Concept sheet full bleed)
  - azoto_logo_clean.webp (Clean logo presentation)

## 4. Environmental & Packaging Design
- **Heading:** Translating Brand to Space
- **Body:** The sharp geometric identity was extended into every physical touchpoint. From the paper cups and tissue paper to the architectural facade of the stores, the brand maintained a consistent, high-contrast aesthetic. The store interiors featured illuminated 3D signage and atmospheric lighting, creating a dramatic stage for the liquid nitrogen ice cream making process.

## 5. Side Project (Pure6)
- **Heading:** Pure6 — The Value of Creative Freedom
- **Body:** Within the intense, fast-paced environment of a startup, maintaining creative balance is crucial. **Pure6** was a non-profit side project developed within UKIN's in-house design team. Centered around a pH6 skincare concept, it provided a space for free artwork exploration and creative confidence building.
- **Body 2:** This side project was more than just a creative outlet — it was a philosophy. Maintaining a balance between input and output inevitably leads to the next step. Though the original Pure6 brand was eventually absorbed into a larger, now-famous brand, the experimental energy it fostered directly influenced the success of flagship projects like AZOTO.
- **Images:**
  - pure6_poster.jpg (Poster)
  - pure6_1.png to pure6_7.png (UI/Artwork screens arranged in a grid)

## 6. Outcome
- **Heading:** Outcome
- **Body:** AZOTO launched successfully, expanding to multiple locations including Hongdae and Anyang. The dramatic brand identity and store experience captured public attention, leading to massive queues and a feature on the major lifestyle TV show *Tasty Road*. The project proved that a strong, cohesive visual language — built from a simple geometric concept to a full spatial experience — can drive tangible business success.
