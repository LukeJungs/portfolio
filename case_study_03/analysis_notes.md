## AZOTO Brand — Key Facts

- **Full name:** AZOTO (아조또) — Liquid Nitrogen Ice Cream
- **Trademark:** 상표/서비스표등록출원 제 45-2014-0004374 (registered 2014)
- **Concept:** Liquid nitrogen ice cream made fresh in front of customers (-196°C)
- **Logo:** Custom geometric mark — ice cream cone shape + triangle/inverted triangle forms = "A" letterform
  - Three concept stages: (1) ice cream cone shape lettering, (2) triangle/inverted triangle structure, (3) outline-emphasized shape variation
- **Identity system:** Black primary, white logo on dark backgrounds; kraft/tan for packaging
- **Applications:** Illuminated store signage, paper cups (BR150cc), tissue paper, store interior, menu boards
- **Locations seen:** Hongdae (홍대점), Anyang (안양점) — multiple franchise locations
- **Media:** Featured on Olive TV "Tasty Road" (테이스티로드) — mainstream food/lifestyle TV show

## AZOTO Narrative

The AZOTO project was a total brand design commission from UKIN. Starting from zero — no name, no identity, no space — the work covered:
1. Logo concept and construction (geometric ice cream cone → custom letterform)
2. Trademark registration (2014)
3. Brand applications: paper cups, tissue paper, packaging
4. Store interior design and signage (illuminated 3D logo, wall graphics)
5. Menu board design
6. Multi-location rollout (Hongdae, Anyang, and more)

The brand achieved mainstream recognition when featured on Olive TV's Tasty Road — a major food lifestyle program in Korea — validating the brand's visual and experiential design.

## Pure6 — Key Facts

- **Brand:** Pure6 (퓨어6)

- **Type:** Non-profit side project within UKIN in-house design
- **Period:** November 2015 (screenshot dates)

## Narrative Theme

The overarching story for this case study:
- UKIN as a design-led startup incubator where Luke was a founding design member
- AZOTO as the flagship project: total brand identity from trademark to physical space
- Pure6 as a side project that nurtured creative confidence — "input/output balance"
- The philosophy: every project, even a side one, feeds the next step
- AZOTO's success (TV feature, multiple locations, queues) validates the design approach
