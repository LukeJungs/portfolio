# Case Study 02: Urban Dive Marketing (UDM)

## 1. Hero Section
- **Title:** Urban Dive Marketing
- **Tags:** Brand Identity · Editorial · Design Leadership
- **Hero Image:** `UDM_2.webp` (Brochure spread composition showing cover, contents, and infographics)
- **Subtitle:** Leading a cohesive brand ecosystem for a business accelerator across Toronto, China, and Pakistan.

## 2. Context Section
- **Heading:** The Challenge
- **Text:** Urban Dive Marketing (UDM) positioned itself as a business accelerator and digital marketing agency based in downtown Toronto. However, its internal visual language lacked the consistency required to build trust with high-value clients. The challenge was not just to redesign a logo or a booklet, but to establish a comprehensive brand ecosystem and typography system. Furthermore, the execution had to be scaled across international teams, requiring robust guidelines and clear design leadership.

## 3. Scope & Leadership Section
- **Heading:** Scope & Leadership
- **Text:** As the Lead Graphic Designer, the role extended far beyond individual deliverables. It involved directing the overarching brand concept, establishing tone and manner, and overseeing all physical and digital touchpoints—from exhibition banners and booth designs to editorial booklets. A critical part of the mandate was mentorship: training interns in the Toronto office and guiding a 20-person design team near the capital of Pakistan to ensure global brand consistency.

## 4. Brand System & Editorial Section
- **Heading:** Establishing the Visual Voice
- **Text:** The core of the identity was codified into a comprehensive brand booklet. This editorial piece served a dual purpose: it was both a client-facing manifesto explaining UDM’s marketing funnel approach, and an internal benchmark for typographic and layout standards. The design utilized a confident, high-contrast palette of deep navy and bright yellow, supported by clean grid systems and bespoke infographics to communicate complex digital strategies clearly.
- **Images:** `UDM_4.webp` (Brand identity grid), `UDM_1.webp` (Booklet stack), `UDM_3.webp` (Intern collaboration spread)

## 5. Collaboration & Expansion Section
- **Heading:** Cross-Brand Collaboration: Sammontana
- **Text:** The UDM ecosystem also involved managing creative collaborations. A notable example was the partnership with Sammontana, an Italian gelati brand. This required adapting UDM’s design sensibilities to a bold, pop-art aesthetic. The artwork collaboration spanned from identity panels to large-format canvas installations and environmental branding within hospitality spaces, demonstrating the versatility of the design leadership.
- **Images:** `sam_2.webp` (Pop art close-up), `sam_5.webp` (Framed posters in lounge), `screenshot_udm.webp` (Interior space with artwork)

## 6. Outcome Section
- **Heading:** Outcome
- **Text:** By centralizing the design vision in Toronto and establishing clear guidelines for international teams, UDM transformed its visual presence. The resulting brand ecosystem successfully communicated their authority as a digital accelerator, while the mentorship structure ensured that every output—from a simple document to a large-scale exhibition booth—adhered to the new standard of excellence.
